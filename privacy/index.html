Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Noodle Inn handles your information when you use this website.

Who we are

Noodle Inn, 156 London Road, Sheffield S2 4LT. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email info@noodleinn.co.uk or call 0114 255 4488.

What we collect and why

This website has no user accounts and does not track you. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and message. We use it solely to reply to your enquiry or booking request. Messages are delivered to our mailbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, and are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

We never sell or share your details with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • No advertising or analytics cookies. We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Language preference. When you switch between English and 中文, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It contains nothing personal and you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.
  • Embedded map. The "Find Us" map on our home page is provided by Google Maps. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and collect usage data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Fonts. Our typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser's IP address is sent to Google to fetch them.
  • Staff sign-in. A session cookie is set only for restaurant staff who sign in to update the menu. Visitors never receive it.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel automatically records basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. These are retained for a short period.

Links to other websites

We link to Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Facebook. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. This version was last updated on 15 September 2026.

關於我們

麵軒 Noodle Inn，156 London Road, Sheffield S2 4LT。如對本政策或您的資料有任何疑問，請電郵至 info@noodleinn.co.uk 或致電 0114 255 4488。

我們收集甚麼資料及原因

本網站沒有用戶帳戶，亦不會追蹤您。我們唯一收到的個人資料，是您透過聯絡表格主動提供的姓名、電郵地址、電話（選填）及訊息。這些資料只會用於回覆您的查詢或訂座要求。訊息由表格轉電郵服務 FormSubmit（formsubmit.co）送達我們的郵箱，並只會保留至處理完您的查詢為止。

我們絕不會將您的資料出售或提供予第三方作推廣用途。

Cookie 及類似技術

  • 沒有廣告或分析 Cookie。本網站不使用任何追蹤、分析或廣告 Cookie。
  • 語言設定。當您切換英文與中文時，您的選擇會儲存在瀏覽器的本機儲存空間，以便網站記住您的偏好。當中不含任何個人資料，您可隨時在瀏覽器設定中清除。
  • 嵌入地圖。主頁「尋找我們」的地圖由 Google 地圖提供。地圖載入時，Google 可能會根據其私隱政策設置其 Cookie 及收集使用資料。
  • 字型。本網站字型由 Google Fonts 提供，瀏覽器會將您的 IP 地址傳送至 Google 以下載字型。
  • 職員登入。只有餐廳職員登入更新餐牌時才會設置一個登入 Cookie，訪客不會收到。

網站寄存

本網站由 Vercel Inc. 寄存。與所有網站寄存服務一樣，Vercel 會自動記錄基本技術日誌（例如 IP 地址、瀏覽器類型及所瀏覽的頁面），用於保安及維持網站運作，並只會短期保留。

其他網站連結

本網站設有 Deliveroo、Uber Eats 及 Facebook 的連結。這些服務各有其私隱政策，我們對其處理您資料的方式概不負責。
